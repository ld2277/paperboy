## STANDING CORRECTIONS - carry these forever
- **AN INSTRUMENT'S TITLE IS NOT ITS SUBJECT AND A SECTION HEADING IS NOT ITS CONTENT. READ THE SECTION.** An EO can impose in section 2 and waive in section 4. A reported number may sit in no published document (ASML's bookings; GF's 7% stake; the China 14nm threshold; Nscale's 85%; the IMF's global core series) - **print the absence, and attribute a calculated figure to whoever calculated it, including us.**
- **A STREAK, ITS LENGTH AND ITS DATE MUST BE CONSISTENT. COUNT SESSIONS AGAINST THE CALENDAR INCLUDING LOCAL HOLIDAYS.**
- **A PACKAGE NUMBER, A BILL NUMBER AND A LEGISLATIVE STAGE COME FROM THE INSTITUTION, NOT A CLIENT ALERT OR A SNIPPET.**
- **A RUNNING SAME-DAY STORY IS A SNAPSHOT. RE-CHECK IT THE NEXT MORNING.** 400 French arrests became ~1,200 that night and ~1,800 by Sunday.
- **TWO OUTLETS CAN BOTH BE RIGHT AND TWO NUMBERS CAN MEASURE DIFFERENT THINGS** - a 552B backbone and a 763B tensor sum; a 13-death strike and a 19-death month; Nunez's 500 injured police and Lecornu's 150. **DO NOT BUILD A CONTRADICTION OUT OF SILENCE OR OUT OF TWO UNITS - BUT DO PRINT AN ARITHMETIC INCONSISTENCY INSIDE ONE SOURCE** (al-Maliki's itemisation sums to 20 against his stated 19).
- **SAY WHICH EVENT A CASUALTY FIGURE COUNTS.**
- **A HEADLINE VERB CAN UNDERSTATE OR OVERSTATE ITS OWN BODY.** "Removes" for "narrows" (Meta); a consultation is not a decision (MSCI/Indonesia); an availability post is not a partnership (Beam on SageMaker); a headline can misattribute an issuer (the ~60bn is Broadcom's).
- **RUN `date -u` FIRST. OCTOBER 2026 FRIDAYS: 2, 9, 16, 23, 30. CHECK WHETHER A NAMED PERSON IS ALIVE - AND THAT AN IMPLAUSIBLE-SOUNDING OFFICIAL IS NOT REAL BEFORE KILLING THE QUOTE.**
- **A QUOTE CIRCULATING IN OUR OWN LOG IS NOT VERIFIED. NEVER ALTER A QUOTE, NEVER SPLICE TWO SENTENCES WITH A CONNECTIVE, AND CHECK THE SPEAKER'S OWN SITE BEFORE REPRINTING LAST WEEK'S.**

- **AN INTRADAY PRINT IS NOT A CLOSE.** `stockanalysis.com/stocks/<ticker>` is the close of record for a US single name; `investing.com/indices/<slug>-historical-data` carries dated daily tables. **NEVER BUILD AN AGGREGATE FROM INTRADAY COMPONENTS, AND LABEL A CLOSE-TO-CLOSE WEEKLY FIGURE AS OUR OWN ARITHMETIC.**
