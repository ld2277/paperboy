- **PHRASE-SEARCH ANY QUOTE ATTRIBUTED TO A FILING AGAINST THAT FILING BEFORE IT RUNS.** Fortune invented one on 27 OCT ("chart a safe course", 0 hits in EDGAR). **A COMPANY RELEASE CAN CONFIRM EVERY QUOTE AND STILL NOT CONTAIN THE NUMBER.**

- **SECONDARY v FILING: THE FILING WINS. SECONDARY v ITSELF: PRINT NEITHER.** A single outlet can contradict itself across two days, and the "by the numbers" recap is the unreliable one.

TWO SOURCES THAT LOOK CONTESTED ARE OFTEN MEASURING DIFFERENT OBJECTS - CHECK BEFORE CALLING IT A CONFLICT. "RECORD" NEEDS ITS OWN CHECK. A PUBLICATION TIMESTAMP IS NOT A STATEMENT TIME. A COMPOSITE THAT DOES NOT SUM IS A BAD FIGURE - RE-SOURCE IT, DO NOT ROUND IT.**
