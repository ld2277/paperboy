## STANDING CORRECTIONS - carry forever
- **A CASE NUMBER IS A CLAIM AND NEEDS ITS OWN VERIFIER.** Never carry a docket number, a judge or a holding forward from a prior log without re-reading a signed order or a court's own docket page - and when a prior issue printed the error, correct it in print. **A COURT'S DOCKET PAGE BEAT ITS OWN ORDER PDF THIS RUN: THE PDF RETURNED A STUB.**
- **A CONDITIONAL IS LOAD-BEARING AND IS THE EASIEST THING TO LOSE.** Check whether the main clause depends on a clause somebody dropped.
- **CHECK THE DOCUMENT'S OWN DATELINE, YEAR, BODY AND WEEKDAY.** An impossible weekday has twice exposed a fabricated framing. One extra fetch of the IR scheduling release is the best check on any earnings claim, and a corporate action like a 4-for-1 split silently corrupts every price page that has not restated. **A PAGE'S LAST-MODIFIED DATE CAN PRECEDE ITS OWN NEWEST ROW - THAT IS A REASON TO DOUBT THE TABLE, NOT THE ROW.**
- **EVERY QUOTE THAT BREAKS, BREAKS THE SAME WAY: A REAL PHRASE LIFTED FROM A LONGER SENTENCE WHOSE OPENING CLAUSE, CONDITIONAL, CAPITAL LETTER OR TRAILING QUALIFIER CHANGED ITS FORCE. PULL THE FULL SENTENCE.** A wire's paraphrase is not a quote; a line with no speaker is narrative; a lowercase first letter means the wire lifted it too; a court's quotation of precedent is not the panel's phrase; a publication's own ellipsis makes a quote spliced; **A TRANSLATION IS NOT A QUOTE - TWO OUTLETS' ENGLISH OF ONE HEBREW SENTENCE DIFFERED THIS RUN, SO NEITHER IS QUOTABLE.**
- **SECONDARY v FILING: THE FILING WINS. SECONDARY v ITSELF: PRINT NEITHER** - a source that contradicts itself within one page is disqualified on that figure. LABEL EVERY NUMBER: INTRADAY v CLOSE, SETTLEMENT v CONTRACT MONTH, GAAP v ADJUSTED, OPERATING v NET, QoQ v YoY, COMMITMENT v CLOSING, PLANNED v PRICED v RAISED, PROJECTION v COUNT, **CONSENSUS FORECAST v PRIOR READING, A MODE SPLIT v A PARTY SPLIT, A SUBSET OF STATES v A NATIONAL FIGURE, ENTERPRISE VALUE v EQUITY VALUE.** "RECORD" NEEDS ITS OWN CHECK - RECORD AMONG WHAT?
- **"UP TO" IS NOT A FLAT AMOUNT. AN LOI IS NOT AN AWARD. A MOTION IS NOT A WIN. A TRO APPLICATION IS NOT A TRO. AN EMERGENCY DENIAL IS NOT A MERITS RULING. A DISMISSAL WITHOUT PREJUDICE IS NOT A MERITS LOSS. A COMMERCIAL AGREEMENT WITH A PRIME IS NOT A GOVERNMENT CONTRACT. A PRODUCTION PLAN IS NOT AN ORDER BOOK. AN ANNOUNCED MEDIATION DATE IS NOT A NEGOTIATION. TALKS ARE NOT A DEAL.**
- **A PARTY'S ACCOUNT OF ITS OWN OPERATIONS IS NOT CONFIRMATION** - CENTCOM's, the IRGC's, IRNA's drone claim, a government's own drone count, the FBI's attribution of its own investigation, a DOJ charge tally, an NGO's own release. **AND THE ABSENCE OF AN ON-THE-RECORD MILITARY STATEMENT IS ITSELF PRINTABLE: SAY SO.**
- **NO COMPANY PRIMARY, NO ITEM** - but a release can be silent on the real cause, and **A RELEASE'S SILENCE IS NOT A SOURCE FOR THE OPPOSITE**: if a figure or a guidance line is not in the release, say it is not in the release rather than sourcing it to the call you did not read. An aggregator is never a primary; a full-text republication is usable but labelled one source removed.
- **A BIOGRAPHY NEEDS ITS OWN VERIFIER AND ITS OWN "DO NOT PRINT" LIST.** Recurring faults: a birth year institutions disagree on; a famous dialogue that is reconstruction from a popular book; a conference the subject never attended; an undocumentable job title; a funeral detail that is one author's unsourced line.
