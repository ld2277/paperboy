PULL THE FULL SENTENCE.** A WIRE'S PARAPHRASE IS NOT A QUOTE; A LINE WITH NO SPEAKER IS NARRATIVE; A LOWERCASE FIRST LETTER MEANS THE WIRE LIFTED IT TOO; ONE QUOTED WORD INSIDE INDIRECT SPEECH IS NOT A QUOTED SENTENCE; A COURT'S QUOTATION OF PRECEDENT IS NOT THE PANEL'S PHRASE; A TRANSLATION CAN TURN A HOPE INTO A FACT. - **SECONDARY v FILING: THE FILING WINS. SECONDARY v ITSELF: PRINT NEITHER.** LABEL EVERY NUMBER: INTRADAY v CLOSE, SETTLEMENT v CONTRACT MONTH, GAAP v ADJUSTED, QoQ v YoY, UNADJUSTED YoY v DAILY-AVERAGE, COMMITMENT v CLOSING, PLANNED v PRICED v RAISED, POPULATION v SAMPLE, PROJECTION v COUNT, **A COMPANY'S OWN "AFFECTED" FIGURE v AN INDEPENDENT ESTIMATE, A GROSS LOSS v A BORNE LOSS, A CHIPS-ONLY BASE v A COMPANY-WIDE ONE.** "RECORD" NEEDS ITS OWN CHECK - ASK "RECORD AMONG WHAT?" A LEAD INSIDE THE MARGIN OF ERROR IS NOT A LEAD; A PREDICTION-MARKET PRICE IS NOT A FORECAST.
- **"UP TO" IS NOT A FLAT AMOUNT. AN LOI IS NOT AN AWARD. A TARGET IS NOT A RECEIPT. AN INITIAL CLOSE IS NOT A CLOSE. A FILING IS NOT A PRICING. AN UNSOLICITED OFFER IS NOT A DEAL. AN FMS DETERMINATION IS A CONGRESSIONAL NOTIFICATION, NOT A SALE. AN OUTLOOK CHANGE IS NOT A DOWNGRADE. A DENIAL OF A MOTION TO DISMISS IS NOT A MERITS WIN. A TRO IS NOT A FINAL JUDGMENT. TALKS ARE NOT A DEAL. A NON-CASH FAIR-VALUE LOSS IS NOT A CASH LOSS.**
- **A PARTY'S ACCOUNT OF ITS OWN OPERATIONS IS NOT CONFIRMATION - CENTCOM'S, THE IRGC'S, THE HOUTHIS' AND THE POLICE'S INCLUDED.** TWO MECHANISMS FOR ONE EVENT IS A REAL CONFLICT; A CUMULATIVE COUNT RISING IS NOT. **AND TWO OFFICIALS OF ONE GOVERNMENT SAYING INCOMPATIBLE THINGS IS A STORY, NOT A SOURCING FAILURE.**

## PAYLOAD - read this before writing a word
**THE OUTBOX WRITE IS ONE `create_file` CALL AND ITS ENTIRE `textContent` MUST BE EMITTED VERBATIM IN A SINGLE MESSAGE, WHICH MEANS IT MUST ALSO BE READ BACK INTO CONTEXT FIRST - SO THE PAYLOAD COSTS ROUGHLY TWICE ITS SIZE IN TOKENS AND IS CAPPED BY SINGLE-MESSAGE OUTPUT LENGTH, NOT BY CONTEXT. drive.google.com, www.googleapis.com AND oauth2.googleapis.com ARE EGRESS-BLOCKED AND gcloud HAS NO CREDENTIALS - IT CANNOT BE PUSHED FROM BASH.**
**HARD SIZE RULE, LEARNED EXPENSIVELY ON 1 NOV: KEEP A WEEKLY UNDER ~80KB AND A DAILY UNDER ~50KB. The first 1 NOV weekly draft assembled at 122KB (11 sections x 4 items x ~230 words) and had to be cut FOUR times - to 114, 107, 90 and finally ~87KB - because the emit risk was unacceptable. 25 OCT weekly 63.7KB; dailies 40-50KB.**
**THE ONLY RELIABLE WAY TO HIT THAT: FIX THE SHAPE BEFORE WRITING A LINE. A WEEKLY IS **2 ITEMS PER TOPIC (3 FOR THE WEEK'S BIGGEST BEAT) AT happened 95 / context 30 / affected 25 / means 55 / watch 15**, i.e. ~23 items and ~5,000 words of items, plus a 750-word top story and an 800-word profile. PUT THOSE FIVE NUMBERS BESIDE EVERY ITEM IN THE DRAFT AND MEASURE AFTER THE FIRST SECTION, NOT THE LAST. Dailies stay at 350-500 a section, happened 90 / means 45 / watch 18. FINISHED COPY RUNS ~9 BYTES A WORD AND THE LOG ADDS 14-18KB.**
**IF A SECTION MUST BE CUT FOR SIZE, CUT WHOLE ITEMS AND LIST THEM UNDER UNPRINTED WITH THEIR VERIFIED FIGURES - NEXT RUN CAN THEN PRINT THEM WITHOUT RE-RESEARCHING. DO NOT CUT context/affected ACROSS THE BOARD; THAT IS WHAT MAKES A WEEKLY A WEEKLY.**
**WARNING ON CUTTING: A NAIVE "DELETE FROM THE HEADLINE TO THE NEXT `]},`" HELPER DELETES EVERY ITEM AFTER THE TARGET IN THAT SECTION TOO. IT SILENTLY ATE TWO GOOD ITEMS THIS RUN. SLICE TO THE NEXT `},{"headline"` INSTEAD, AND ALWAYS PRINT THE SURVIVING HEADLINE LIST AFTER ANY CUT.**
