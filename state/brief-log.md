## STANDING CORRECTIONS - carry these forever
- **AN INSTRUMENT'S TITLE IS NOT ITS SUBJECT. ASK WHICH DOCUMENT ACTUALLY DID THE THING, AND READ IT.** A reported number may sit in no published document (ASML and bookings; GF's 7% stake) - print the absence. **BUT THE OPPOSITE HAPPENS TOO: THE CALIFORNIA 20,000 IS PLEADED FOUR TIMES, AND A DOJ RELEASE THAT NAMES NO COURT USUALLY HAS A COMPANION USAO RELEASE AND AN UNLINKED COMPLAINT PDF. HUNT BEFORE DECLARING AN ABSENCE.**
- **A HEADLINE VERB CAN UNDERSTATE ITS OWN BODY** ("agrees" over "adopted and published"). **AND THE REVERSE: A CONSULTATION IS NOT A DECISION** (MSCI/Indonesia).
- **RUN `date -u` BEFORE WRITING THE RESEARCH BRIEF. OCTOBER 2026 FRIDAYS: 2, 9, 16, 23, 30.** An outlet's weekday can be wrong while its date is right. **CHECK WHETHER A NAMED PERSON IS ALIVE.**
- **CHECK THE DATE ON THE VENDOR'S OWN PAGE FOR EVERY ITEM AN AGGREGATOR HANDS YOU.** Stale datelines arrive in batches.
- **AN INTRADAY PRINT IS NOT A CLOSE - THE MOST FREQUENT FAULT IN THIS LOG.** AP's Asia levels, Korea JoongAng's screen captions, Korea Times' FX prints, Benzinga's same-day percentages and fortune.com's oil snapshots are all intraday. **NEVER BUILD AN AGGREGATE FROM INTRADAY COMPONENTS.**
- **DO NOT MERGE CONFERENCE SPEAKERS.** On TSMC's call Wei gave the growth raise and the Terafab answer; Huang gave capex; the release quotes only Huang.
- **A PARTY'S ACCOUNT OF ITS OWN OPERATIONS IS NOT CONFIRMATION - CENTCOM'S, THE IDF'S, THE IRGC'S, THE TPF'S AND ERITREA'S ALIKE.** A preliminary rolling count is not a rival tally.
- **NEVER SILENTLY ALTER A QUOTE, AND CHECK IT IS FROM THIS WINDOW.**
- **A SECTOR INDEX CLAIM NEEDS ITS OWN SOURCE** - use single names instead.
- **A COURT'S CAPTION, A JUDGE'S SPELLING, A VOTE'S SHAPE AND THE COUNT OF COUNTS COME FROM THE PDF, NOT THE RELEASE.** St. Paul draws two counts and Portland one from the same release.
