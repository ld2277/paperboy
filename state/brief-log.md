## STANDING CORRECTIONS - carry forever
- **CHECK THE DOCUMENT'S OWN DATELINE, AND THE YEAR AND BODY ON ANY SET-PIECE. A PLAUSIBLE URL, A CORRECT FIGURE SET AND A VERIFIED QUOTE CAN ALL SIT INSIDE A DOCUMENT NINE MONTHS OLD.** Three of the four hardest errors on 29 OCT were DATE errors on REAL events. One extra fetch of the IR scheduling release or the 8-K exhibit description is the highest-value check on any earnings claim.
- **EVERY QUOTE THAT BREAKS, BREAKS THE SAME WAY: A REAL PHRASE LIFTED OUT OF A LONGER SENTENCE WHOSE SUBORDINATE CLAUSE, CAPITAL LETTER OR TRAILING QUALIFIER CHANGED ITS FORCE.** Pull the full sentence from the source document, not the clause from the coverage. **PHRASE-SEARCH ANY QUOTE ATTRIBUTED TO A FILING AGAINST THAT FILING. A RELEASE CAN CONFIRM EVERY QUOTE AND STILL NOT CONTAIN THE NUMBER - OR CONTAIN NO GUIDANCE AT ALL.**
- **SECONDARY v FILING: THE FILING WINS. SECONDARY v ITSELF: PRINT NEITHER. LABEL WHAT A NUMBER IS: INTRADAY v CLOSE, SETTLEMENT v CONTRACT MONTH, TOTAL v VALID VOTES, GAAP v ADJUSTED, EV v DEAL PRICE, CUMULATIVE v ONE DAY, CARRIED-OVER GUIDANCE v FRESH. TWO SOURCES THAT LOOK CONTESTED ARE OFTEN MEASURING DIFFERENT OBJECTS. "RECORD" NEEDS ITS OWN CHECK, AND A COMPANY'S OWN RECORD CLAIM IS AN ATTRIBUTION, NOT A FACT. A COMPOSITE THAT DOES NOT SUM IS A BAD FIGURE - RE-SOURCE IT.**
- **A WIRE'S PARAPHRASE IS NOT A QUOTE, AND A WIRE PRINTS IT IN SENTENCE FORM THAT READS EXACTLY LIKE ONE. A PARTY'S STATEMENT IS NOT ITS LEADER'S QUOTE. A TRANSLATION CAN CONVERT A HOPE INTO A FACT. AN ELLIPSIS OR A BRACKET INSIDE A FOUR-WORD QUOTE MEANS IT IS STITCHED - DROP IT.**
- **"UP TO" IS NOT A FLAT AMOUNT. AN INDICATION OF INTEREST IS NOT A PURCHASE. A PRESENTED PACKAGE IS NOT A LAW. AN LOI IS NOT AN AWARD. "NARROWED" IS NOT "RAISED". STATUS DISCIPLINE - CONVICTION, CHARGE, ARREST, ALLEGATION, DISMISSAL, WITHDRAWAL, ANNOUNCED INTENTION: NEVER LET ONE READ AS ANOTHER. A DENIAL OF A TRO IS NOT A MERITS RULING, AND NEITHER IS A JURISDICTIONAL DISMISSAL.**
- **A PARTY'S ACCOUNT OF ITS OWN OPERATIONS IS NOT CONFIRMATION** - CENTCOM's, the Houthis', HUR's and the Ukrainian Air Force's included - **BUT A MINISTRY'S OWN COUNT CAN UNDERCUT ITS OWN PRIME MINISTER, AND THAT IS PRINTABLE.**
