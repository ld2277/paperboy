## STANDING CORRECTIONS - carry forever
- **CHECK THE DOCUMENT'S OWN DATELINE, AND THE YEAR AND BODY ON ANY SET-PIECE. ONE EXTRA FETCH OF THE IR SCHEDULING RELEASE OR THE 8-K EXHIBIT DESCRIPTION IS THE HIGHEST-VALUE CHECK ON ANY EARNINGS CLAIM.**
- **EVERY QUOTE THAT BREAKS, BREAKS THE SAME WAY: A REAL PHRASE LIFTED OUT OF A LONGER SENTENCE WHOSE SUBORDINATE CLAUSE, CAPITAL LETTER OR TRAILING QUALIFIER CHANGED ITS FORCE.** Pull the full sentence from the source document. **PHRASE-SEARCH ANY QUOTE ATTRIBUTED TO A FILING AGAINST THAT FILING. A RELEASE CAN CONFIRM EVERY QUOTE AND STILL NOT CONTAIN THE NUMBER. A LINE IN A RELEASE WITH NO SPEAKER IS NARRATIVE, NOT A QUOTE. A LOWERCASE FIRST LETTER MEANS THE WIRE LIFTED IT TOO.**
- **SECONDARY v FILING: THE FILING WINS. SECONDARY v ITSELF: PRINT NEITHER. LABEL WHAT A NUMBER IS: INTRADAY v CLOSE v MID-DAY LAST TRADE, SETTLEMENT v CONTRACT MONTH, TOTAL v VALID VOTES, GAAP v ADJUSTED (THEY CAN POINT OPPOSITE WAYS - INTEL), QoQ v YoY, EV v EQUITY VALUE, GROSS v NET-OF-PROCEEDS, CARRIED-OVER GUIDANCE v FRESH ("we continue to expect" IS THE TEXTUAL EVIDENCE), RELEASE v CALL. TWO SOURCES THAT LOOK CONTESTED ARE OFTEN MEASURING DIFFERENT OBJECTS. "RECORD" NEEDS ITS OWN CHECK, AND A COMPANY'S OWN RECORD CLAIM IS AN ATTRIBUTION.
