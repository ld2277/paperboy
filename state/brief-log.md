## STANDING CORRECTIONS - carry forever
- **CHECK THE DOCUMENT'S OWN DATELINE, AND THE YEAR AND BODY ON ANY SET-PIECE: A PLAUSIBLE URL, A CORRECT FIGURE SET AND A VERIFIED QUOTE CAN ALL SIT INSIDE A DOCUMENT TWO YEARS OLD, AND A WEEKEND SEARCH WILL SURFACE LAST YEAR'S DECISION AT THE TOP. **CHECK THE WEEKDAY TOO - "FRIDAY 31 OCTOBER" WAS IMPOSSIBLE AND THAT IS WHAT EXPOSED THE LAVROV FRAMING.** ONE EXTRA FETCH OF THE IR SCHEDULING RELEASE IS THE HIGHEST-VALUE CHECK ON ANY EARNINGS CLAIM.**
- **EVERY QUOTE THAT BREAKS, BREAKS THE SAME WAY: A REAL PHRASE LIFTED OUT OF A LONGER SENTENCE WHOSE SUBORDINATE CLAUSE, CONDITIONAL, CAPITAL LETTER OR TRAILING QUALIFIER CHANGED ITS FORCE. PULL THE FULL SENTENCE FROM THE SOURCE.** FOUR OF FIVE QUOTES CHECKED ON 1 NOV BROKE THIS WAY, AND SO DID A FIGURE. A WIRE'S PARAPHRASE IS NOT A QUOTE. A LINE WITH NO SPEAKER IS NARRATIVE. A LOWERCASE FIRST LETTER MEANS THE WIRE LIFTED IT TOO. **A SINGLE QUOTED WORD INSIDE INDIRECT SPEECH IS NOT A QUOTED SENTENCE.** A COURT'S QUOTATION OF PRECEDENT IS NOT THE PANEL'S OWN PHRASE. A TRANSLATION CAN TURN A HOPE INTO A FACT, AND TWO OUTLETS CAN RENDER ONE SENTENCE TWO WAYS.
- **SECONDARY v FILING: THE FILING WINS. SECONDARY v ITSELF: PRINT NEITHER. AND CHECK WHICH WAY ROUND - A PRESS REPORT CAN CARRY A FIGURE THE FILING LACKS WHILE OMITTING ONE IT HAS. LABEL WHAT A NUMBER IS: INTRADAY v CLOSE, SETTLEMENT v CONTRACT MONTH, GAAP v ADJUSTED, QoQ v YoY, **UNADJUSTED YoY v DAILY-AVERAGE**, EV v EQUITY, COMMITMENT v CLOSING, **PLANNED v PRICED v RAISED**, FLASH v REVISED, **A POPULATION v A SAMPLE DRAWN FROM IT**, **A PROJECTION v A COUNT**. "RECORD" NEEDS ITS OWN CHECK AND IS USUALLY THE TRAP - ASK "RECORD AMONG WHAT?" A COMPOSITE THAT DOES NOT SUM IS A BAD FIGURE. AN OUTLOOK CHANGE IS NOT A DOWNGRADE. A DENIAL OF A MOTION TO DISMISS IS NOT A WIN ON THE MERITS.** A SPECIAL MASTER IS NOT A RULING. A JURISDICTIONAL DISMISSAL IS NOT A MERITS RULING.
