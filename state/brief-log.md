## STANDING CORRECTIONS - carry these forever
- **AN INSTRUMENT'S TITLE IS NOT ITS SUBJECT, AND THE REAL INSTRUMENT MAY NOT BE A PRESIDENTIAL ACTION AT ALL. ALWAYS ASK WHICH DOCUMENT ACTUALLY DID THE THING, AND READ IT. AND A REPORTED NUMBER MAY SIT IN NO PUBLISHED DOCUMENT AT ALL - BEFORE PRINTING A POLICY FIGURE, CHECK THE ISSUING BODY'S OWN NEWS PAGE AND SAY SO IF IT IS ABSENT. NEW.**
- **CHECK THE DAY OF THE WEEK BEFORE WRITING THE RESEARCH BRIEF - RUN `date -u` FIRST. OCTOBER 2026 FRIDAYS: 2, 9, 16, 23, 30. AND CHECK WHETHER A NAMED PERSON IS ALIVE: A QUOTE THAT READS ODDLY USUALLY MEANS A FACT YOU DO NOT HAVE.**
- **NAME THE STAGE EVERY TIME: A VOLUNTARY GLOBAL PLEDGE IS NOT A NATIONAL TARGET; A SHARED UNDERSTANDING IS NOT A SIGNED STATEMENT AND NEITHER IS A TABLED LEGAL TEXT; PLENARY APPROVAL IS NOT COUNCIL ADOPTION; A COMMITTEE REJECTION IS NOT A DEFEAT OF A BUDGET; A COMPLAINT IS NOT A FINDING; A PROPOSAL IS NOT A LAW; **EARLY TALKS ARE NOT A DEAL AND THREE COMPETING STRUCTURES ARE NOT A STRUCTURE**.**
- **WHERE TWO GOVERNMENTS DESCRIBE THE SAME ARRANGEMENT INCOMPATIBLY - ONE BRIEFING A CUT, THE OTHER ANNOUNCING GROWTH - PRINT BOTH AND THE ABSENCE OF A TEXT. DO NOT PICK. NEW.**
- **A COURT ORDER'S TYPE MATTERS: A TRO IS NOT A PRELIMINARY INJUNCTION IS NOT A VACATUR IS NOT SUMMARY JUDGMENT, A TENTATIVE RULING IS NOT A RULING, A HEARING IS NOT A RULING, **A PROVISIONAL HABEAS GRANT WITH TIME FOR THE GOVERNMENT TO RESPOND IS NOT A RELEASE**, AND **AN APPLICATION FOR INTERIM RELIEF IS NOT A MERITS JUDGMENT**. AN ORDER CAN BE TWO THINGS AT ONCE (A TRO *AND* A 5 U.S.C. 705 STAY) - READ THE OPERATIVE PARAGRAPHS. SAY "NO RULING LOCATED", NEVER "NO RULING ISSUED". **TRY storage.courtlistener.com/recap BEFORE CONCLUDING NO DOCKET IS OBTAINABLE, AND TAKE THE CAPTION FROM THE ORDER - A NAMED LAW FIRM OR GROUP MAY NOT BE A PARTY.**
- **A PRESIDENTIAL MEMORANDUM IS NOT AN EXECUTIVE ORDER AND DESIGNATES **OFFICES**, NOT PEOPLE - AND ONE PERSON CAN HOLD TWO. A TASK FORCE, CZAR OR BILL ANNOUNCED ON SOCIAL MEDIA IS NOT AN INSTRUMENT. AN EO ON whitehouse.gov IS "SIGNED"; ONLY AN FR DOC NUMBER MAKES IT "PUBLISHED". AN EO THAT DIRECTS AN AGENCY TO DECIDE HAS NOT DECIDED.**
- **A DEADLINE RUNS FROM THE DATE THE INSTRUMENT GIVES, NOT FROM PUBLICATION, AND A QUALIFIER CHANGES IT. A CONSTITUTIONAL CLOCK HAS ITS OWN ARITHMETIC - GET IT FROM THE INSTITUTION'S OWN CALENDAR DOCUMENT. SAY "NO DETERMINATION LOCATED", NEVER "NONE ISSUED".**
- **A COMPANY MAY NAME ITS OWN MODEL, PRODUCT OR FIGURE WHERE THE TRADE PRESS DOES NOT - BEFORE WRITING "THE COMPANY DECLINED TO SAY", READ THE PRIMARY, AND CHECK /research AS WELL AS /news. AND A CENTRAL BANK NAMES ITS OWN INSTRUMENTS: GET THE RESERVE'S NAME, THE CIRCULAR NUMBER AND THE SCOPE FROM THE CIRCULAR, NOT FROM THE WIRE. NEW.**
- **A ROUNDUP ONE-LINER IS A LEAD, NOT A SOURCE. AN OUTLET'S RUNNING TALLY IS NOT AN OFFICIAL COUNT - NAME WHOSE COUNT IT IS. AN OUTLET'S OWN CONDITIONAL ARITHMETIC ATTRIBUTED TO NOBODY IS THE WRITER'S SUM.**
- **A QUOTE CROPPED AT A COMMA OR A CONJUNCTION IS A CROPPED QUOTE; AN ELLIPSIS MUST NOT BRIDGE TWO SENTENCES; A QUOTE STRIPPED OF ITS HEDGE IS A CHANGED QUOTE; A HEADLINE PHRASE IS NOT A QUOTE. **QUOTE ORDER IS PART OF THE QUOTE - CHECK WHICH SENTENCE CAME FIRST.
