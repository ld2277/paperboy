## STANDING CORRECTIONS - carry these forever
- **AN INSTRUMENT'S TITLE IS NOT ITS SUBJECT. ASK WHICH DOCUMENT DID THE THING, AND READ IT.** A reported number may sit in no published document (ASML and bookings; GF's 7% stake; the China 14nm chip threshold) - print the absence. **BUT HUNT FIRST: THE CALIFORNIA 20,000 IS PLEADED FOUR TIMES, AND A DOJ RELEASE NAMING NO COURT USUALLY HAS A COMPANION USAO RELEASE AND AN UNLINKED COMPLAINT PDF. AND A FIGURE CAN SIT IN AN ANNEX YOU CANNOT OPEN - SAY SO AND ATTRIBUTE IT.**
- **TWO OUTLETS CAN BOTH BE RIGHT.** "Does not name specific models" and "the annex carries thresholds" are compatible; so are a midday print and a close. **DO NOT BUILD A CONTRADICTION OUT OF ONE SOURCE'S SILENCE, AND CHECK BOTH DATELINES BEFORE CALLING AN OUTLET SELF-CONTRADICTORY.**
- **A HEADLINE VERB CAN UNDERSTATE ITS OWN BODY** ("agrees" over "adopted and published"). **AND THE REVERSE: A CONSULTATION IS NOT A DECISION** (MSCI/Indonesia), **AN AVAILABILITY POST IS NOT A PARTNERSHIP** (Beam on SageMaker; NVIDIA under RAISE), **AND A HEADLINE CAN MISATTRIBUTE OWNERSHIP** (Jev is TypeSafe's, not Vercel's).
- **RUN `date -u` BEFORE WRITING THE RESEARCH BRIEF. OCTOBER 2026 FRIDAYS: 2, 9, 16, 23, 30.** An outlet's weekday can be wrong while its date is right. **CHECK WHETHER A NAMED PERSON IS ALIVE.**
- **CHECK THE DATE ON THE PRIMARY'S OWN PAGE FOR EVERY ITEM AN AGGREGATOR HANDS YOU.** Stale datelines arrive in batches: republished court stories, an eight-month-old capex forecast, a three-month-old approval, a week-old IPO withdrawal, release notes read as a launch.
- **AN INTRADAY PRINT IS NOT A CLOSE - STILL THE MOST FREQUENT FAULT, BUT DO NOT OVERCORRECT.** Benzinga's same-day percentages, fortune.com's and Quartz's index numbers, Axios's episode figures (CoreWeave -15.5% against a -11.91% close) and **The Standard's unlabelled mainland line** are intraday. **AP'S ASIA LEVELS WERE CORRECT ON 16 OCT - VERIFY RATHER THAN ASSUME EITHER WAY.** `stockanalysis.com/stocks/<ticker>` is the close of record for a US single name. **NEVER BUILD AN AGGREGATE FROM INTRADAY COMPONENTS.**
