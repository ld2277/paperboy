## STANDING CORRECTIONS - carry these forever
- **AN INSTRUMENT'S TITLE IS NOT ITS SUBJECT. ASK WHICH DOCUMENT DID THE THING, AND READ IT.** An EO can impose in section 2 and waive in section 4 - say "imposed and simultaneously waived", never "did not impose". A reported number may sit in no published document (ASML and bookings; GF's 7% stake; the China 14nm chip threshold; **Nscale's 85% concentration**) - print the absence and attribute a calculated figure to whoever calculated it. **BUT HUNT FIRST: THE CALIFORNIA 20,000 IS PLEADED FOUR TIMES, AND A FIGURE CAN SIT IN AN ANNEX YOU CANNOT OPEN.**
- **A RUNNING SAME-DAY STORY IS A SNAPSHOT. RE-CHECK ITS NUMBERS THE NEXT MORNING.** Al Jazeera's 400 French arrests became Lecornu's 1,200 after its filing time.
- **TWO OUTLETS CAN BOTH BE RIGHT, AND TWO NUMBERS CAN MEASURE DIFFERENT THINGS.** "Does not name specific models" and "the annex carries thresholds" are compatible; so are a 552B backbone and a 763B tensor sum; so are a 12-death strike and a 17-death month. **DO NOT BUILD A CONTRADICTION OUT OF ONE SOURCE'S SILENCE OR OUT OF TWO UNITS.**
- **SAY WHICH EVENT A CASUALTY FIGURE COUNTS.** A campaign of repeated strikes compressed into "a strike" manufactures a false dispute.
- **A HEADLINE VERB CAN UNDERSTATE OR OVERSTATE ITS OWN BODY.** "Agrees" for "adopted and published"; **"removes" for "narrows"** (Meta's terms); a consultation is not a decision (MSCI/Indonesia); an availability post is not a partnership (Beam on SageMaker); a headline can misattribute ownership (Jev is TypeSafe's) or an issuer (**the ~60bn is BROADCOM'S raise, not Anthropic's**).
- **RUN `date -u` BEFORE WRITING THE RESEARCH BRIEF. OCTOBER 2026 FRIDAYS: 2, 9, 16, 23, 30.** An outlet's weekday can be wrong while its date is right. **CHECK WHETHER A NAMED PERSON IS ALIVE - AND, EQUALLY, CHECK THAT AN IMPLAUSIBLE-SOUNDING OFFICIAL IS NOT REAL BEFORE KILLING THE QUOTE.** Appointed successors exist.
- **CHECK THE DATE ON THE PRIMARY'S OWN PAGE FOR EVERY ITEM AN AGGREGATOR HANDS YOU, AND DO NOT MISTAKE A MODIFIED DATE FOR A PUBLICATION DATE.** Stale datelines arrive in batches: republished court stories, old capex forecasts, three-month-old approvals, Anthropic research pages dated 16 OCT and modified 17 OCT, and site headers printing today's date above yesterday's articles (techstartups).
- **AN INTRADAY PRINT IS NOT A CLOSE - BUT DO NOT OVERCORRECT.** Benzinga's same-day percentages, fortune.com's and Quartz's index numbers and The Standard's unlabelled mainland line are intraday; **AP'S ASIA LEVELS WERE CORRECT ON 16 OCT**. `stockanalysis.com/stocks/<ticker>` is the close of record for a US single name. **NEVER BUILD AN AGGREGATE FROM INTRADAY COMPONENTS.**
- **DO NOT MERGE CONFERENCE SPEAKERS, AND A CEO ONSTAGE IS NOT THE COMPANY.** On TSMC's call Wei gave the growth raise and Huang the capex. Masad's Replit-Anthropic claim was contradicted inside the same article, and TechCrunch later changed the headline to "makes OpenAI its default". **NEVER WRITE "REPLIT ENDED ITS ANTHROPIC PARTNERSHIP".**
- **A PARTY'S ACCOUNT OF ITS OWN OPERATIONS IS NOT CONFIRMATION - CENTCOM'S, THE IDF'S, THE IRGC'S, THE SAUDI COALITION'S, THE TPF'S AND ERITREA'S ALIKE. UKMTO, ACLED AND KPLER ARE THE USABLE THIRD PARTIES IN THE GULF AND THE HORN.** And a claim can grow between runs with no new event behind it.
- **NO COMPANY PRIMARY, NO ITEM, WHEN THE CLAIM IS ABOUT THE COMPANY ITSELF.** A breach, a partnership or a cancellation sourced only to a blocked social post or a single paper is attributable at best and usually droppable.
- **NEVER SILENTLY ALTER A QUOTE, AND NEVER SPLICE TWO SENTENCES INTO ONE WITH A CONNECTIVE.** Adding "because" between two remarks invents a causal claim the speaker did not make. A quote can also be an AI-translated paraphrase of an old call, or a paraphrase with no original.
