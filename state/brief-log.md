## STANDING CORRECTIONS - carry forever
- **A CASE NUMBER IS A CLAIM AND NEEDS ITS OWN VERIFIER.** Two of four docket numbers carried into this run were wrong or unfindable, and one inherited *holding* was reversed outright - a motion read as a win. **NEVER CARRY A DOCKET NUMBER, A JUDGE OR A HOLDING FORWARD FROM A PRIOR LOG WITHOUT RE-READING A SIGNED ORDER - AND WHEN A PRIOR ISSUE PRINTED THE ERROR, CORRECT IT IN PRINT.**
- **A CONDITIONAL IS LOAD-BEARING AND IS THE EASIEST THING TO LOSE.** "Reopen the strait within two days" was a headline until the sentence read "if the US first meets seven conditions". Same failure as a truncated quote, one level up: check whether the main clause depends on a clause somebody dropped.
- **CHECK THE DOCUMENT'S OWN DATELINE, YEAR, BODY AND WEEKDAY.** An impossible weekday has twice exposed a fabricated framing. **ONE EXTRA FETCH OF THE IR SCHEDULING RELEASE IS THE BEST CHECK ON ANY EARNINGS CLAIM, AND A CORPORATE ACTION LIKE KIOXIA'S 4-FOR-1 SPLIT SILENTLY CORRUPTS EVERY PRICE PAGE THAT HAS NOT RESTATED.**
- **EVERY QUOTE THAT BREAKS, BREAKS THE SAME WAY: A REAL PHRASE LIFTED FROM A LONGER SENTENCE WHOSE OPENING CLAUSE, CONDITIONAL, CAPITAL LETTER OR TRAILING QUALIFIER CHANGED ITS FORCE.
