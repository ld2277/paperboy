## STANDING CORRECTIONS - carry these forever
- **AN INSTRUMENT'S TITLE IS NOT ITS SUBJECT, AND THE REAL INSTRUMENT MAY NOT BE A PRESIDENTIAL ACTION AT ALL. ALWAYS ASK WHICH DOCUMENT ACTUALLY DID THE THING, AND READ IT. A REPORTED NUMBER MAY SIT IN NO PUBLISHED DOCUMENT AT ALL - BEFORE PRINTING A POLICY OR DEAL FIGURE, CHECK THE ISSUING BODY'S OWN PAGE OR THE FILINGS THEMSELVES AND SAY SO IF IT IS ABSENT. A COMPANY MAY DECLINE TO PUBLISH THE METRIC EVERYONE EXPECTS (ASML AND BOOKINGS) - PRINT THE ABSENCE. **AND A COMPANY'S OWN RELEASE MAY OMIT THE MOST-QUOTED PARTS OF ITS OWN STORY (GF: NO 7% STAKE, NO PRIORITY QUOTE, NO COMPENSATION) - PRINT THE FILING AND ATTRIBUTE THE REST TO THE WIRE.****
- **A HEADLINE VERB CAN UNDERSTATE ITS OWN BODY. "AGREES" IN A COUNCIL HEADLINE OVER "adopted ... published in the Official Journal" IN THE BODY MEANS ADOPTED AND PUBLISHED. READ THE BODY BEFORE SOFTENING A STAGE.**
- **CHECK THE DAY OF THE WEEK BEFORE WRITING THE RESEARCH BRIEF - RUN `date -u` FIRST. OCTOBER 2026 FRIDAYS: 2, 9, 16, 23, 30. AN OUTLET'S OWN WEEKDAY CAN BE WRONG WHILE ITS DATE IS RIGHT - TRUST THE DATE AND THE PRIMARY. AND CHECK WHETHER A NAMED PERSON IS ALIVE: A QUOTE THAT READS ODDLY USUALLY MEANS A FACT YOU DO NOT HAVE.**
- **NAME THE STAGE EVERY TIME: A VOLUNTARY GLOBAL PLEDGE IS NOT A NATIONAL TARGET; A SHARED UNDERSTANDING IS NOT A SIGNED STATEMENT AND NEITHER IS A TABLED LEGAL TEXT; PLENARY APPROVAL IS NOT COUNCIL ADOPTION; A COMMITTEE REJECTION IS NOT A DEFEAT OF A BUDGET; A COMPLAINT IS NOT A FINDING; A PROPOSAL IS NOT A LAW; A SANCTIONS DESIGNATION IS NOT A COURT FINDING AND A RELEASE ON BAIL WITHOUT CHARGE IS NOT A CHARGE; AN ARTICLE 7 HEARING IS NOT A FINDING; EARLY TALKS ARE NOT A DEAL; **AND THE OPENING OF A TRIAL IS NOT A VERDICT, A REPORTED DENIAL IS NOT A PLEA, AND A DISMISSAL MOTION IS NOT A RULING.****
- **WHERE TWO GOVERNMENTS DESCRIBE THE SAME ARRANGEMENT INCOMPATIBLY - ONE ANNOUNCING A CEASEFIRE, THE OTHER REFUSING A PAUSE - PRINT BOTH AND THE ABSENCE OF A TEXT. DO NOT PICK.**
- **A COURT ORDER'S TYPE MATTERS: A TRO IS NOT A PI IS NOT A VACATUR IS NOT SUMMARY JUDGMENT, A TENTATIVE RULING IS NOT A RULING, A HEARING IS NOT A RULING, A PROVISIONAL HABEAS GRANT WITH TIME TO RESPOND IS NOT A RELEASE, AND AN APPLICATION FOR INTERIM RELIEF IS NOT A MERITS JUDGMENT. AN ORDER CAN BE TWO THINGS AT ONCE (A TRO *AND* A 5 U.S.C. 705 STAY). A MEMORANDUM OPINION MAY BE NO ORDER AT ALL AND SAY "a separate order shall issue" - **BUT AN *OPINION OF THE COURT* CAN ALSO CARRY ITS OWN DECRETAL SENTENCE ("we will affirm"), SO READ TO THE END BEFORE DECIDING WHICH IT IS.** COUNT THE GROUNDS, READ FOR THE BOND (A NOMINAL $1 IS STILL A BOND) AND FOR A DENIED STAY, CHECK WHETHER RELIEF IS AS-APPLIED OR FACIAL AND WHO IS *EXCLUDED*. SAY "NO RULING LOCATED", NEVER "NO RULING ISSUED". **TAKE THE CAPTION, THE DOCKET NUMBER, EVERY JUDGE'S SPELLING AND THE VOTE LINE-UP FROM THE PDF - COVERAGE INVENTS CAPTIONS FROM OFFICIALS' NAMES, MISSPELLS JUDGES, AND REPORTS A TALLY THE OPINION DOES NOT STATE. "CONCURRING IN PART AND DISSENTING IN PART" HIDES WHETHER THE JUDGE SUPPLIED THE MAJORITY: LOOK FOR "concurring in the judgment". AND AN OPINION'S INTRODUCTION CAN MISDESCRIBE ITS OWN FACTS SECTION.** DOCKET *METADATA* PARTIES MAY BE CORPORATE-DISCLOSURE FILERS. TWO RELATED CASES BEFORE THE SAME JUDGE HAVE DIFFERENT NUMBERS.**
- **A PRESIDENTIAL MEMORANDUM IS NOT AN EXECUTIVE ORDER AND DESIGNATES OFFICES, NOT PEOPLE - AND ONE PERSON CAN HOLD TWO. A TASK FORCE, CZAR OR BILL ANNOUNCED ON SOCIAL MEDIA IS NOT AN INSTRUMENT. AN EO ON whitehouse.gov IS "SIGNED"; ONLY AN FR DOC NUMBER MAKES IT "PUBLISHED" - AND FR PUBLICATION LAGS SIGNING BY DAYS, SO **whitehouse.gov, NOT THE FR, IS THE LIVE SOURCE FOR PROVING AN ABSENCE**. AN EO THAT DIRECTS AN AGENCY TO DECIDE HAS NOT DECIDED. AN FR NOTICE PUBLISHED TODAY MAY COVER AN ACTION ISSUED DAYS EARLIER - READ THE "This action was issued on" LINE. **SANCTIONS TAGS ARE PER-ENTRY, NOT PER-TRANCHE, AND OFAC PUBLISHES ONE PAGE PER ACTION WITH `_NN` SUFFIXES - A BARE DATE PAGE IS NOT THAT DAY'S FULL LIST. A DELETION IS AN ACTION TOO.****
- **AN INTERIM FINAL RULE IS NEITHER A PROPOSED NOR A FINAL RULE: IT CAN BE EFFECTIVE ON PUBLICATION *AND* OPEN FOR COMMENT. NAME EVERY WAIVER IT INVOKES (553(b)(B) GOOD CAUSE, 553(a)(1) FOREIGN AFFAIRS, 553(d)(3) DELAYED-EFFECTIVE-DATE) - OUTLETS REPORT ONE WHERE THE PREAMBLE INVOKES THREE. AND TAKE THE AFFECTED-POPULATION FIGURE FROM THE RULE'S OWN SCOPE STATEMENT, NOT FROM A PAST-PROGRAMME NUMBER ELSEWHERE IN THE PREAMBLE.**
- **A DEADLINE RUNS FROM THE DATE THE INSTRUMENT GIVES, NOT FROM PUBLICATION, AND A QUALIFIER CHANGES IT. A CONSTITUTIONAL CLOCK HAS ITS OWN ARITHMETIC. SAY "NO DETERMINATION LOCATED", NEVER "NONE ISSUED".**
- **A COMPANY NAMES ITS OWN MODEL, PRODUCT, PRICE AND FIGURE - READ THE PRIMARY, AND KNOW THAT THE PRIMARY MAY NOT BE ON THE OBVIOUS BLOG. CHECK research AND dev SUBDOMAINS, THE CLOUD BLOG AND THE **API DOCS CHANGELOG**. A MODEL CARD'S OWN TABLE CAN CONTRADICT THE TRADE PRESS'S READING - WHEN ROW ALIGNMENT IS UNCERTAIN, KILL THE CLAIM. WIRE COPY ABOUT A LAUNCH CARRIES EMBEDDED CLAIMS - CUSTOMER NAMES, FREE-ACCESS PROGRAMMES, TIER AVAILABILITY, A CANCELLED PRIOR MODEL - THAT THE VENDOR'S PAGES OFTEN DO NOT SUPPORT. CHECK EACH SEPARATELY. **AND A VENDOR'S OWN VALUATION, PRICE AND PARTICIPATION-vs-PARTNERSHIP FRAMING BEAT EVERY AGGREGATOR'S: "THROUGH" AN INITIATIVE IS NOT "WITH" IT.** A CENTRAL BANK ALSO NAMES ITS OWN INSTRUMENTS.**
- **A ROUNDUP ONE-LINER IS A LEAD, NOT A SOURCE - AND AN AGGREGATOR DIGEST WILL RE-DATE A STALE ANNOUNCEMENT TO TODAY. CHECK THE DATE ON THE VENDOR'S OWN PAGE FOR EVERY ITEM IT HANDS YOU. AN OUTLET'S RUNNING TALLY IS NOT AN OFFICIAL COUNT. AN OUTLET'S CONDITIONAL ARITHMETIC ATTRIBUTED TO NOBODY IS THE WRITER'S SUM.**
- **A QUOTE CROPPED AT A COMMA OR A CONJUNCTION IS A CROPPED QUOTE; AN ELLIPSIS MUST NOT BRIDGE TWO SENTENCES; A QUOTE STRIPPED OF ITS HEDGE IS A CHANGED QUOTE; A HEADLINE PHRASE IS NOT A QUOTE. **A CEO'S STATEMENT IS USUALLY FIVE SENTENCES AND COVERAGE PRINTS THE FIRST - IF YOU RUN ONE SENTENCE OF IT, MARK THE CROP.** A PHRASE MAY EXIST ONLY AS A SUBORDINATE CLAUSE INSIDE A LONGER QUOTE. A QUOTE'S TENSE AND ORDER ARE PART OF IT. TWO OUTLETS CAN EACH CARRY A REAL BUT DIFFERENT TRUNCATED FRAGMENT OF ONE STATEMENT - GO TO THE ISSUING RELEASE. IF A QUOTE IS NOT IN THE FETCHED PAGE, IT DOES NOT EXIST - AND A PHRASE ATTRIBUTED TO AN INSTRUMENT MUST BE FOUND IN *THAT* INSTRUMENT.**
- **A PARAPHRASE OF A TRANSLATED STATEMENT IS SAFER THAN A QUOTE, AND AN OFFICIAL TRANSLATION'S SPELLING IS PART OF THE QUOTE. **AN OUTLET WHOSE ENGLISH EDITION IS AI-MACHINE-TRANSLATED (view.asiae.co.kr SAYS SO) IS USABLE BUT MUST BE PAIRED.** A SOCIAL-MEDIA POST IS NOT A SPOKEN STATEMENT AND THE REVERSE ALSO BITES; x.com AND truthsocial.com WILL NOT FETCH, SO CITE THE OUTLET THAT READ IT - AND IF THE OUTLET NAMES NO CHANNEL, NAME NO CHANNEL. A COMPANY'S NEWSROOM POST CAN EXIST ONLY ON X.**
- **A PERSON'S NAME AND TITLE ARE FACTS WITH A SINGLE AUTHORITY. TAKE A DEFENDANT'S SPELLING, AGE, NATIONALITY AND JOB DESCRIPTION FROM THE CHARGING RELEASE OR THE ORDER. **TWO GOOD OUTLETS CAN DISAGREE ON WHICH SERVICE A DEFENDANT SERVED IN (ARMY vs SECURITY SERVICE), AND ONE MAY PRINT A FULL NAME THE COURT ANONYMISES.****
- **A HIRE-AND-LICENSE IS NOT AN ACQUISITION AND A TEAM JOINING A COMPANY IS NOT AN ACQUISITION; IN A JOINT ACQUISITION THE PERIMETER IS JOINT UNLESS THE RELEASE SPLITS IT. A GOVERNMENT RELEASE MAY NOT NAME THE CAUSE EVERYONE KNOWS. A GOVERNMENT CONTRACT AWARD IS PROCUREMENT REVENUE, NOT FUNDING RAISED; **A COMPUTE DONATION VALUED AT $X IS NOT $X OF CASH; AND A STATE EQUITY STAKE IS NOT A SUBSIDY.****
- **A TIMEZONE IS PART OF A TIME. DATE EVERY CASUALTY FIGURE, NAME WHOSE COUNT IT IS, AND NEVER SPLICE TWO DAYS' PAIRS. **A PRELIMINARY ROLLING COUNT FROM ONE OFFICIAL IS NOT A RIVAL TALLY TO ANOTHER'S FINAL - SETTING THEM AGAINST EACH OTHER MANUFACTURES A CONTRADICTION. AND WHEN A SUB-COUNT'S RELATION TO THE TOTAL IS NOT STATED, ASSERT NEITHER.** FRENCH "INTERPELLATIONS" ARE NOT ARRESTS.**
- **NO PARTY'S ACCOUNT OF ITS OWN OPERATIONS IS CONFIRMATION - CENTCOM, SOUTHCOM, the IDF, the Russian MoD, the Ukrainian general staff and air force, Taiwan's MND, the Houthis and any state agency alike. WHERE TWO SIDES GIVE INCOMPATIBLE TERRITORIAL CLAIMS, PREFER THE ONE THAT PUBLISHES ITS GEOLOCATION METHOD AND SAY SO. AN ADVANCE REPORTED ONLY BY THE ADVANCING GOVERNMENT IS A CLAIM; A TOWN TAKEN "WITHOUT A FIGHT" ON RESIDENT TESTIMONY IS WITNESS REPORTING. **AND A WIRE'S "six sources" CHARACTERISATION IS ITSELF A FACT WORTH CARRYING - IT IS WHAT MAKES THE CLAIM BETTER THAN A COMMUNIQUE.****
- **ARITHMETIC RECONCILIATION IS NOT VERIFICATION - CONFIRM A LEVEL AGAINST A SECOND INDEPENDENT SOURCE, NEVER AGAINST ITS OWN DELTA. CAUSATION IS A FACT AND NEEDS VERIFYING LIKE A NUMBER. A SECTOR FRAMING IS A CLAIM - CHECK EVERY CONSTITUENT, AND **A SECTOR *INDEX* LEVEL NEEDS ITS OWN SOURCE: A SINGLE BAD OUTLET'S -4.3% CAN BE THE ONLY CARRIER.** TWO OUTLETS AGREEING CAN STILL BE WRONG IF BOTH ARE THE OUTLIERS - PREFER SOURCES THAT PRINT THE POINT CHANGE ALONGSIDE THE PERCENTAGE AND COUNT HOW MANY INDEPENDENT OUTLETS BACK EACH VERSION. A SECOND DISPATCH FROM THE SAME WIRE IS NOT A SECOND OUTLET. WHEN THREE GOOD OUTLETS GIVE IRRECONCILABLE PRICE/PERCENT PAIRS FOR ONE STOCK, PRINT WHAT THEY AGREE ON AND OMIT THE REST.**
- **AN INTRADAY LEVEL IS NOT A CLOSE, AND IN A FAST TAPE THE GAP CAN BE 1.5 PERCENTAGE POINTS. **WIRE MARKET STORIES ABOUT ASIA ARE WRITTEN AND UPDATED MID-SESSION: THEIR PERCENTAGES CAN BE RIGHT WHILE THEIR LEVELS RUN LOW, AND TWO CARRIERS OF THE SAME STORY HOLDING DIFFERENT NUMBERS IS THE PROOF. GO TO A DOMESTIC OUTLET FOR AN ASIAN CLOSE. A SCREEN CAPTION IN A DOMESTIC PAPER CAN ALSO BE INTRADAY.** A DATA VENDOR'S WEEKLY TABLE MAY BE AN INTRADAY SNAPSHOT, AND A VENDOR'S DAY-CHANGE FIELD CAN BE WRONG WHILE ITS LEVEL IS RIGHT. A SPREAD CAN NARROW BECAUSE THE OTHER LEG ROSE. TWO INDEXES IN ONE MARKET CAN CLOSE IN OPPOSITE DIRECTIONS. AN INDEX CAN RISE WHILE ITS LARGEST CONSTITUENT IS UNCHANGED - THAT IS A ROTATION, AND SAYING SO IS THE STORY. **A SECTOR ROTATION *WITHIN* A CRASHING INDEX (TAIPEI FINANCIALS +0.73% ON A -3.41% DAY) IS THE SAME STORY.****
- **A MARKET PRICE FROM A DATA VENDOR IS NOT A SETTLEMENT - WRITE "ENDED". A PMI, PRICE OR YIELD NEEDS ITS INDEX, UNIT, CONTRACT MONTH AND TENOR. A PAGE THAT PRINTS A YIELD WHILE SAYING THE BOND MARKET WAS CLOSED IS SELF-REFUTING. ISM SERVICES IS NOT S&P GLOBAL SERVICES. A SUB-INDEX IS NOT A HEADLINE, AND CHECK WHAT A LOW PRINT IS LOW *SINCE*. A CENTRAL BANK'S POLICY RATE HAS A NAME. **A STATISTICAL RELEASE MAY PRINT THE SAME COMPONENT TWICE, SEASONALLY ADJUSTED AND NOT (BLS GASOLINE 9.1% SA / 6.5% NSA) - NEVER CROSS THEM, AND CHECK THE PRIOR MONTH BEFORE CALLING A PRINT A STEP-UP.****
- **GAAP VS NON-GAAP TRAPS RECUR EVERY SEASON, AND A COMPANY CAN REAFFIRM ONE GUIDANCE WHILE RAISING THE OTHER. A FORWARD FIGURE IN A CEO'S QUOTE IS STILL COMPANY GUIDANCE; A MULTI-YEAR PROJECTION STATED AT A CONFERENCE AND ABSENT FROM THE RELEASE IS STILL THE COMPANY'S - SAY WHERE IT CAME FROM, AND **NAME WHICH EXECUTIVE SAID WHICH NUMBER: THE CEO AND THE CFO SPLIT THE SCRIPT, AND THE RELEASE MAY QUOTE ONLY ONE OF THEM.** A SELL-SIDE PREVIEW IS NEVER A COMPANY NUMBER. A REPORTED SHARE REACTION IS NOT A CLOSE AND A PREMARKET MOVE IS NOT A DAY'S OUTCOME. TWO BANKS CAN USE DIFFERENT NAMES FOR THE SAME LINE - CARRY EACH BANK'S OWN LABEL. A HEADLINE RELEASE MAY GIVE ONLY A PERCENTAGE WITH THE DOLLAR FIGURE IN THE SUPPLEMENT (Ex-99.2). A SECONDARY'S "X, UP Y%" CAN PAIR THE GROWTH RATE WITH THE *YEAR-AGO BASE*. **AND A SECONDARY CAN PRINT THE TOP OF A *REVENUE* GUIDANCE RANGE AS *CAPEX*. A RETURN METRIC CAN FALL WHILE REVENUE AND EPS RISE - CHECK THE PRIOR-YEAR ROTCE BEFORE CALLING A BEAT AN IMPROVEMENT.****
- **CUMULATIVE FUNDING IS NOT A ROUND SIZE; A VALUATION ATTACHES TO ONE ROUND; A RUN-RATE OR ARR IS NOT REVENUE; A FOUNDER'S FIGURE IS NOT A CONTRACT; A CONDITIONAL GOVERNMENT LOAN COMMITMENT IS NOT FUNDING RAISED; CAPITAL RAISED *INTO FUNDS* IS NOT CAPITAL DEPLOYED *INTO COMPANIES*; AND **A DEBT FINANCING IS NOT AN EQUITY ROUND, AN UPSIZE IS NOT A NEW FACILITY, AND A "RESERVATION" A COMPANY EXPLICITLY EXCLUDES FROM ITS OWN BOOKINGS IS NOT A BOOKING.** WHERE AN OUTLET STATES NEITHER PRE- NOR POST-MONEY, SUPPLY NEITHER. AN ONGOING ROUND IS NOT A CLOSED ONE. A DEAL VALUE THAT APPEARS ONLY IN COVERAGE AND IN NO FILING IS NOT A NUMBER. A TOTAL ACROSS TWO ROUNDS ANNOUNCED TOGETHER IS NOT A ROUND. **AND A SEPARATE SIMULTANEOUS ANNOUNCEMENT (DAYONE'S IPO REGISTRATION ALONGSIDE ITS FINANCING) MUST NOT BE MERGED INTO THE ONE YOU ARE WRITING ABOUT - NOR CALLED ABSENT WHEN IT EXISTS ELSEWHERE.****
- **AN ELECTION PERCENTAGE OR SEAT COUNT IS A SNAPSHOT OF A COUNT. A POLL NEEDS ITS SAMPLE, MoE, FIELD DATES, SPONSOR AND REGISTRATION; A HYPOTHETICAL FROM A PARTISAN IS NOT A POLL; A POLL-AVERAGE SITE IS NOT A RELEASE.**
- **THREE WIRES ON ONE ANONYMOUS STORY ARE NOT THREE CONFIRMATIONS - SAY "REPORTED". A WIRE STORY CARRIED BY FOUR SMALL OUTLETS IS ONE SOURCE. A TRADE OUTLET WHOSE OWNER INVESTED IN THE SUBJECT MUST BE CITED WITH THAT DISCLOSURE. A SECOND-HAND CHAIN MUST BE PRINTED AS SUCH. A NUMBER YOU NEVER FETCHED CANNOT BE HEDGED INTO PRINT - DROP THE ITEM.**
- **AN ARTICLE'S DATELINE IS NOT ITS SUBJECT'S DATE, AND A PLAUSIBLE URL MAY BE YEARS OLD. A SAME-DAY-TIMESTAMPED PIECE MAY DESCRIBE THE *PREVIOUS* SESSION, AND A DAILY SLUG MAY CARRY A YEAR-TO-DATE CUMULATIVE. FOR A FUNDING ROUND OR BANKRUPTCY FILING, FIND THE ORIGINAL ANNOUNCEMENT DATE. **A WIRE'S URL SLUG DATE CAN ALSO BE A DAY LATER THAN THE VENDOR'S OWN POST.** courthousenews.com SERVES A STALE HEADER, pbs.org/newshour CAN SERVE PRIOR-YEAR AP COPY, dockets.justia.com SNAPSHOTS CAN BE WEEKS STALE, AND senate.gov's floor_activity PAGE IS NOT A LIVE FEED. AN OFFICIAL BODY'S OWN SITE CAN BE STALE, GEO-BLOCKED OR A JS SHELL - WHEN IT IS, SAY "NOT VERIFIED FROM AN OFFICIAL BODY" AND NAME THE STRONGEST SECONDARY.**
