## STANDING CORRECTIONS - carry forever
- **A CASE NUMBER IS A CLAIM AND NEEDS ITS OWN VERIFIER.** Never carry a docket number, a judge or a holding forward from a prior log without re-reading a signed order or a court's own docket page. A court's docket page has beaten its own order PDF.
- **A CONDITIONAL IS LOAD-BEARING AND IS THE EASIEST THING TO LOSE.** Check whether the main clause depends on a clause somebody dropped.
- **CHECK THE DOCUMENT'S OWN DATELINE, YEAR, BODY AND WEEKDAY.** An impossible weekday has twice exposed a fabricated framing. A page's last-modified date can precede its own newest row - that is a reason to doubt the table, not the row. **A LIVE BLOG FETCHED TODAY MAY SERVE YESTERDAY'S CACHE: THE CBS AND YAHOO ELECTION LIVE PAGES CAME BACK PRE-RESULTS ON 4 NOV WHILE THEIR SEARCH TITLES SAID "DEMOCRATS WIN".**
- **EVERY QUOTE THAT BREAKS, BREAKS THE SAME WAY: A REAL PHRASE LIFTED FROM A LONGER SENTENCE WHOSE OPENING CLAUSE, CONDITIONAL, CAPITAL LETTER OR TRAILING QUALIFIER CHANGED ITS FORCE. PULL THE FULL SENTENCE.** A wire's paraphrase is not a quote; a line with no speaker is narrative; a lowercase first letter means the wire lifted it too; a publication's own ellipsis makes a quote spliced; **A TRANSLATION IS NOT A QUOTE - AND TWO TRANSCRIPTS OF ONE INTERVIEW ARE NOT ONE QUOTE EITHER.**
- **SECONDARY v FILING: THE FILING WINS. SECONDARY v ITSELF: PRINT NEITHER.** LABEL EVERY NUMBER: INTRADAY v CLOSE v AFTER-HOURS, SETTLEMENT v CONTRACT MONTH, GAAP v ADJUSTED, **RELEASE v CALL**, OPERATING v NET, QoQ v YoY, COMMITMENT v CLOSING, PLANNED v PRICED v RAISED, PROJECTION v COUNT, CONSENSUS FORECAST v PRIOR READING, A MODE SPLIT v A PARTY SPLIT, A SUBSET OF STATES v A NATIONAL FIGURE, ENTERPRISE VALUE v EQUITY VALUE, **CONTESTS WON v SEATS HELD, A MEDIA PROJECTION v A CERTIFIED RESULT, A LEAD v A WIN, A CONCESSION v A CALL.** "RECORD" NEEDS ITS OWN CHECK - RECORD AMONG WHAT?
- **"UP TO" IS NOT A FLAT AMOUNT. AN LOI IS NOT AN AWARD. A MOTION IS NOT A WIN. A TRO APPLICATION IS NOT A TRO. AN EMERGENCY DENIAL IS NOT A MERITS RULING. A DISMISSAL WITHOUT PREJUDICE IS NOT A MERITS LOSS. A PRODUCTION PLAN IS NOT AN ORDER BOOK. AN ANNOUNCED MEDIATION DATE IS NOT A NEGOTIATION. TALKS ARE NOT A DEAL. AN AGENDA ITEM IS NOT A PASSED LAW. A DECLARED INTENTION TO RECOMMEND IS NOT A RECOMMENDATION.**
- **A PARTY'S ACCOUNT OF ITS OWN OPERATIONS IS NOT CONFIRMATION** - CENTCOM's, the IRGC's, a government's own drone count, the FBI's attribution of its own investigation, a DOJ charge tally, an NGO's own release, **a company's own characterisation of a lawsuit it filed**. **AND THE ABSENCE OF AN ON-THE-RECORD MILITARY STATEMENT IS ITSELF PRINTABLE - BUT ONLY AS "NONE FOUND IN COVERAGE REVIEWED" IF THE MILITARY'S OWN SITE WOULD NOT LOAD.**
