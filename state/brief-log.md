## STANDING CORRECTIONS - carry forever
- **A CASE NUMBER IS A CLAIM AND NEEDS ITS OWN VERIFIER.** Never carry a docket number, judge or holding forward without re-reading a signed order or the court's own docket page. **A FEDERAL JUDGE'S OWN WEEKLY CALENDAR PDF CAN BEAT EVERY OUTLET: cod.uscourts.gov GAVE US A DOCKET NUMBER NOBODY HAD PRINTED.**
- **A CONDITIONAL IS LOAD-BEARING AND THE EASIEST THING TO LOSE. "PRIMARILY", "UP TO", "AT LEAST", "EXPECTED" AND "LIKELY" ALL COUNT.**
- **CHECK THE DOCUMENT'S OWN DATELINE, YEAR, BODY AND WEEKDAY.** An impossible weekday has twice exposed a fabricated framing. **A LIVE BLOG FETCHED TODAY MAY SERVE A CACHE HOURS OR MONTHS OLD - THE MISSISSIPPI FREE PRESS LIVEBLOG WAS 13 HOURS STALE AND AN UNDATED UN PRESS STATEMENT TURNED OUT TO BE FEBRUARY. PULL THE FULL SENTENCE.** A wire's paraphrase is not a quote; a line with no speaker is narrative; a lowercase first letter means the wire lifted it too; **A TRANSLATION IS NOT A QUOTE AND TWO TRANSLATIONS OF ONE STATEMENT ARE NOT TWO SOURCES. A TYPO IN THE ONLY PUBLISHED COPY IS NOT A QUOTE EITHER.**
